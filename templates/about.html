How AegisCare Works

AegisCare is an MCA-level healthcare identity platform that retrieves patient records only after role login and biometric verification. It combines face and fingerprint matching, encrypted biometric storage, patient medical records, AI-generated health risk screening, and a tamper-evident audit ledger.

During registration, patient demographics, clinical profile, face image, and fingerprint image are stored. During retrieval, OpenCV processes uploaded biometrics and compares them with encrypted enrolled samples. In multimodal mode, both face and fingerprint must satisfy the matching thresholds before the record opens.

The AI layer predicts risk from vitals such as BP, glucose, pulse, temperature, SpO2, BMI, symptoms, and latest diagnosis. The blockchain layer hashes major events into a linked ledger, so the examiner can see how audit records become tamper-evident. The FHIR-style JSON export demonstrates interoperability with external healthcare systems.
